What does £299 include?
  • One responsive page with up to six agreed sections.
  • Your existing logo, colours and up to eight approved images.
  • Light editing of up to 600 words of supplied content, plus a page title and description.
  • One booking-request form with up to five fields, delivered to your nominated inbox.
  • Mobile and desktop checks, one consolidated revision round and launch on your existing hosting.

We check hosting compatibility before accepting the project. The exact payable price, VAT treatment and payment terms are confirmed in your quote before you commit.

What do I need to supply?

Your business details, approved logo and images, service information and up to 600 words of content. We can reuse material from your current website with your approval.

We also need the receiving inbox, approved privacy and business disclosures, secure access to your hosting, and one person to collect feedback and approve the page. Please do not email passwords.

How do revisions work?

One collected round of wording, image, colour and minor layout changes is included. Corrections to our mistakes do not use that allowance.

Extra rounds, new pages or a new design direction are agreed and priced before any additional work. Aim to send feedback within five business days; later replies are scheduled into the next available slot.

How long will it take?

Our proposed target is five UK business days to a completed page for review, then up to two for the included revisions and launch after approval.

Your actual dates are confirmed before you commit, once we have checked hosting, agreed an available start date and received access and approved content. Waiting for content or feedback pauses the timetable.

Does the form confirm a booking?

It sends a booking request to your business inbox. You confirm the appointment with the customer. It does not reserve a calendar slot automatically.

We test that requests arrive before handoff. Clinical forms are limited to administrative contact and appointment requests, without medical details. The design preview is a concept, not a live booking system.

Can I add more later?

Yes. Once your page is built and signed off, we can discuss hosting, CRM, booking or voice agents, extra pages and bespoke features that suit your needs.

Everything is optional and separately scoped. You can keep the £299 page as a standalone purchase.
