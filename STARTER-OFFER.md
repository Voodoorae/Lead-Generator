# Your one-page business website: £299

See a personalised design preview before deciding to go ahead. Your finished page uses your current domain and hosting, your approved branding and a form for customers to request a booking. Hosting and CRM upgrades are optional.

## What is included

- One responsive page with up to six sections: introduction, services, about your business, supplied project images or approved testimonials, booking requests and contact details.
- Your existing logo, brand colours and up to eight approved images. Testimonials are included only when you supply approved, attributable wording.
- Light editing of up to 600 words of supplied business content, plus a page title and description.
- One booking-request form with up to five agreed fields, delivered to your nominated business inbox. The standard fields are name, email, optional phone, service requested and preferred date/time. Requests require your confirmation; the form does not reserve an appointment automatically.
- Mobile and desktop checks, form validation and appropriate spam protection supported by the agreed hosting setup, a test request confirmed as received, and deployment on your existing hosting.
- One consolidated revision round before launch and a short handoff explaining how booking requests arrive.

Clinical forms ask for administrative contact and appointment-request details only. They do not request symptoms, medical history or treatment information.

6. One person who can approve the content and provide a single collected list of changes.

One round means one collected list of changes to the agreed page: wording within the content allowance, supplied image swaps, colours and minor layout adjustments. Send your feedback within five business days of receiving the build for review, where possible.

Corrections to our mistakes and defects in the agreed functionality do not use your revision allowance. Additional rounds, new pages, a different design direction or new functionality are separately scoped before any extra work starts. Feedback received later is scheduled into the next available slot, with no automatic extra charge solely for replying late.
